    "## 3. Feature Engineering\n",
    "\n",
    "We engineer five new features, chosen because they build directly on relationships the\n",
    "Week 1 EDA identified as important (overtime/workload, income, tenure, satisfaction, age):\n",
    "\n",
    "1. **AverageSatisfaction**, a single composite of the four related satisfaction scores\n",
    "   (EnvironmentSatisfaction, JobSatisfaction, RelationshipSatisfaction, WorkLifeBalance),\n",
    "   reducing redundancy between four correlated ordinal measures.\n",
    "2. **TenureRatio**, proportion of an employee's total working life spent at this company\n",
    "   (`YearsAtCompany / TotalWorkingYears`), capturing loyalty/stability better than either\n",
    "   raw number alone.\n",
    "3. **IncomePerExperienceYear**, monthly income divided by total working years, a rough\n",
    "   proxy for whether pay matches experience level.\n",
    "4. **AgeGroup**, bins Age into the same brackets used in Week 1 (18-25, 26-35, 36-45,\n",
    "   46-55, 56-60), since attrition risk was shown to be non-linear across age rather than\n",
    "   a smooth trend.\n",
    "5. **PromotionStagnationRatio**, years since last promotion relative to years at the\n",
    "   company, flagging employees who may feel overlooked for advancement."
